About VEYA-AI
unofficial Vencord build
What is VEYA-AI?

A build of the Discord client mod Vencord with an AI built in: AI chat that reads the open channel, right-click actions, a writing helper, a server builder and server analysis. Plugins and themes are managed through Vencord as usual.

Use at your own risk

VEYA-AI is unofficial and is neither supported nor reviewed by Discord. Client mods violate Discord's Terms of Service – in the worst case your account can be banned.

Privacy

API keys are stored with Windows encryption – only your Windows account can read them. Messages to VEYA go only to the AI provider you choose. There's no VEYA server in between.
